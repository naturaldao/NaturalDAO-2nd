## O01 · Sven Dorkenwald and colleagues; FlyWire Consortium (2024), Fig. 1

果蝇脑连接组测到的是什么。a呈现中央脑与两侧视叶的神经元重建；b–d列出电镜、分割、脑区和分析资源；e显示昆虫一个突触前结构可连接多个突触后对象，因此突触链接数不同于神经元对图边数；f为电镜中的结构实例。原图对应成年雌性脑，未包含完整腹神经索；连接结构也不等于完整运行机制。原图：Dorkenwald等，Nature（2024），Fig.1，CC BY 4.0；未改动。

出处：*Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain*，[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y)，[官方图页](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07558-y/figures/1)。许可：[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[许可及图注核读入口](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11446842/)。原图未改动。

结论边界：图是脑连接组，不能当全动物中枢；不直接展示所有连接的生理权重、调质与活体动态。

## O02 · Peter Mussells Pires, Lingwei Zhang, Victoria Parache, L. F. Abbott and Gaby Maimon (2024), Fig. 1

当前朝向与目标相关状态可以被分开编码。a–e显示转向问题、中央复合体结构及虚拟环境；h–i比较EPG与FC2群体活动；j–o在视觉条纹突然旋转时，EPG活动峰随之移动，而FC2活动峰相对稳定。图支持两类群体携带不同信息，不能仅凭本图认定FC2已经完成全部目标选择。原图：Mussells Pires等，Nature（2024），Fig.1，CC BY 4.0；未改动。

出处：*Converting an allocentric goal into an egocentric steering signal*，[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-07006-3)，[官方图页](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-07006-3/figures/1)。许可：[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[许可及图注核读入口](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10881393/)。原图未改动。

结论边界：Fig.1是记录与扰动视觉参照的证据；FC2因果操纵和PFL3转向作用需结合原论文其他图，不能说本图证明全部导航闭环。

## O03 · Peter Mussells Pires, Lingwei Zhang, Victoria Parache, L. F. Abbott and Gaby Maimon (2024), Fig. 3

PFL3的输出同时依赖当前朝向和目标角度。a概括输入与左右转向通路；b展示行走果蝇的膜片钳记录；c–d显示朝向调谐；e–f表明，按目标角度分组后，同一朝向下的放电率也会改变。f中的细线/小点为单细胞调谐，大圆为群体均值，粗线为模型拟合。图支持信号结合，模型拟合不等于全部转向机制已被证明。原图：Mussells Pires等，Nature（2024），Fig.3，CC BY 4.0；未改动。

出处：*Converting an allocentric goal into an egocentric steering signal*，[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-07006-3)，[官方图页](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-07006-3/figures/3)。许可：[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[许可及图注核读入口](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10881393/)。原图未改动。

结论边界：生理记录与拟合不能单独建立完整行为因果链，也不能把一个PFL3细胞说成独立完成全部动作决策。

## O04 · Yoshinori Aso and colleagues (2014), Figure 1

蘑菇体怎样把感觉信息、强化信号和输出通道组织在一起。A中灰线为Kenyon细胞轴突，彩色区室按MBON递质类别编码，红/绿箭头标示多巴胺通路与惩罚/奖赏关联；B展示MBON类型与投射的分层关系。此图是概括解剖与已有功能证据的原论文线路图，不是完整突触连接组，也不表示每个MBON直接控制一块肌肉。原图：Aso等，eLife（2014），Figure 1，CC BY 4.0；未改动。

出处：*Mushroom body output neurons encode valence and guide memory-based action selection in Drosophila*，[DOI](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.04580)，[官方图页](https://elifesciences.org/articles/04580/figures#fig1)。许可：[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[许可及图注核读入口](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4273436/)。原图未改动。

结论边界：图1本身主要是综合线路图；MBON接近/回避的因果效应及学习结果应引用论文相应行为实验，不能仅凭线路图推定全部功能。

## O05 · Janne K. Lappalainen and colleagues (2024), Fig. 3

同一连接约束与任务优化仍可产生不同模型解。a–b把50个视觉模型对T4c的预测分为向上选择、向下选择和无方向选择等簇；c–e联系相关输入线路与调谐差异。与已知T4c方向偏好相符的簇任务误差较低，但其他解仍存在。图说明模型集能提出待检验机制，而不是仅凭连接图就唯一恢复真实脑。原图：Lappalainen等，Nature（2024），Fig.3，CC BY 4.0；未改动。

出处：*Connectome-constrained networks predict neural activity across the fly visual system*，[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07939-3)，[官方图页](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07939-3/figures/3)。许可：[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[许可及图注核读入口](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07939-3#rightslink)。原图未改动。

结论边界：图中多数结果是模型输出，黑线等已知比较来自实验文献；不能将全部曲线当作此次活体测量，也不能称模型复刻了完整果蝇。

## O06 · Antoine Wystrach, Florent Le Moël, Leo Clement, Sebastian Schwarz (2026), Figure 1

原文图1：熟悉路线上的视觉场景可提供目标在左侧或右侧的信息。图中含实拍蚂蚁、实验布局与不同朝向下的行为结果；陌生场景作为对照。

出处：*Updating an allocentric goal from lateralised egocentric visual memories*，[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-67545-3)，[官方图页](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13096645/figure/Fig1/)。许可：[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[许可及图注核读入口](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13096645/)。原图未改动。

结论边界：This is behavioral evidence and an author photograph, not direct neural imaging. Two species are studied; neural localization to mushroom bodies and central complex remains partly inferred. Do not turn this into evidence of universal ant navigation or a fully measured ant brain circuit.

## O07 · Yann S. Dufour, Xiongfei Fu, Luis Hernandez-Nunez, Thierry Emonet (2014), Figure 1

原文图1：细菌趋化中的感觉模块与鞭毛运动模块通过CheY-P耦合；示意图比较固定细胞的阶跃刺激、持续变化刺激和自由运动时的行为反馈。运动会改变后续输入，因此感觉适应必须在闭环中理解。

出处：*Limits of Feedback Control in Bacterial Chemotaxis*，[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003694)，[官方图页](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article/figure?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003694.g001)。许可：[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[许可及图注核读入口](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003694)。原图未改动。

结论边界：The panels are pathway/response schematics from a study using analytical models and simulations; do not label the plotted traces as new experimental recordings or infer universal optimality. The paper has a 2014 correction affecting equations and some parameter labels; the correction does not replace Figure 1.

## O08 · Emiri Yoneoka, Atsuko Takamatsu (2023), Figure 5

原文图5：多头绒泡菌在奎宁条件下跨越连接区域的速度，按同日对照平均速度归一化；重复经历与后续管网处理条件下的行为差别用于研究习惯化。

出处：*Relation between learning process and morphology of transport tube network in plasmodium of Physarum polycephalum*，[DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2023.1249165)，[官方图页](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10667701/figure/F5/)。许可：[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[许可及图注核读入口](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cell-and-developmental-biology/articles/10.3389/fcell.2023.1249165/full)。原图未改动。

结论边界：The graph is a specific behavioral task and does not establish universal cellular learning or a complete molecular memory mechanism. Day 5 groups differ in network handling/orientation; preserve group labels and consult the full original caption for statistical conventions. The authors explicitly state that no direct evidence establishes the proposed thickness-oscillation pumping mechanism and that morphology alone is insufficient to explain behavior; nutrient and retained-chemical factors remain.

## O09 · Emiri Yoneoka, Atsuko Takamatsu (2023), Figure 7

原文图7：奎宁条件下第1至第4天的管网实拍示例、主要方向随时间变化及方向分布。作者以这些形态指标研究运输管网变化与习惯化的关系；它与图5的行为量度互为补充。

出处：*Relation between learning process and morphology of transport tube network in plasmodium of Physarum polycephalum*，[DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2023.1249165)，[官方图页](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10667701/figure/F7/)。许可：[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)；[许可及图注核读入口](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cell-and-developmental-biology/articles/10.3389/fcell.2023.1249165/full)。原图未改动。

结论边界：Images are representative examples, not all replicates. Correlation of morphology with habituation does not by itself identify a molecular storage mechanism; this paper is distinct from Kramar and Alim 2021 and must not be attributed to that study. The authors explicitly state that no direct evidence establishes the proposed thickness-oscillation pumping mechanism and that morphology alone is insufficient to explain behavior; nutrient and retained-chemical factors remain.
